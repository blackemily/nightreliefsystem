Sleep Revive: What the Seller Says

A neutral inventory of product details presented on the official sales page.

Five named ingredients

The official Sleep Revive sales page identifies rutaecarpine, lemon balm extract, valerian root, L-theanine, and magnesium glycinate. The page describes the product as a capsule supplement and presents the ingredients as a formula intended for sleep support.

Capsule count and directions shown online

The sales page states that a bottle contains 60 vegetarian capsules and that the seller's directions are two capsules 30 to 45 minutes before bed. This is a statement about the seller's webpage, not personal medical advice. Read the directions on the product you actually receive because labels can change.

What the public page does not establish

The searchable sales-page text reviewed for this guide did not provide verified amounts per serving for each ingredient. It also did not present a published randomized clinical trial of the finished Sleep Revive formula in the materials reviewed here. Ingredient studies cited by a seller are not the same as a trial of the complete product.

Guarantee and purchasing terms

The sales page advertises a 60-day guarantee. Review the current support and returns terms before purchasing, including how to request a return, whether return shipping is required, and how the deadline is calculated. Keep the order confirmation and packaging.
